Executive interpretation

Social visibility increased significantly in August Meta views increased from 23,505 in July to 37,258 in August (+58.5%). August included two paid Meta campaigns — one supporting retail volunteer recruitment and one for Gift in Will activity in the final week of the month — which contributed to the uplift in visibility.
Social engagement also strengthened Engagement increased from 661 in July to 2,096 in August (+217%), indicating stronger interaction with social content alongside the increased visibility.
More Meta views came from people outside the existing follower base Non-follower views increased from 80.4% in July to 87.6% in August. The paid campaigns in August helped broaden exposure beyond existing followers.
Website traffic remained stable Website sessions moved from 5,018 in July to 5,155 in August (+2.7%). Direct traffic increased its share while Organic Search remained a major acquisition channel.
Email performance should be read in the context of campaign mix July included Hospice Happenings and Winter Appeal communications, while August included regular Hospice Happenings alongside more targeted IPU Development and Kāpiti donor communications. This difference in audience, purpose and message mix means the overall open-rate movement should not be interpreted as a simple decline in email performance.
Key takeaway: August showed stronger digital visibility and interaction, particularly across social channels. Website demand remained steady, while the email mix shifted from broader appeal activity in July to more targeted supporter communications in August.

Reporting context

Email context: July's email mix included Hospice Happenings and Winter Appeal communications. August included Hospice Happenings plus targeted IPU Development and Kāpiti donor updates. Overall open rates therefore reflect different audience groups and campaign objectives.
Social context: August Meta performance was supported by two paid campaigns — a retail volunteer recruitment campaign and a Gift in Will campaign launched in the final week of August. This should be considered when comparing August Meta views and non-follower visibility with July.
Definitions
Meta views = total content views, not unique people reached. Social engagement = interactions reported by Meta. Website sessions = GA4 sessions. Non-follower views = share of Meta views generated by people who do not follow the page. Email open rate should be interpreted alongside campaign purpose and audience composition.
